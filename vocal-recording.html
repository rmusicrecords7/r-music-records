BEFORE THE SESSION

Prepare the song and vocal direction

Vocal recording is for artists who have a song or instrumental ready and need lead vocals, doubles, backing vocals, or additional takes. Send the material before booking so the session can be planned around the song.

01

Song material. Send an instrumental or demo in the best available quality. If you have a rough vocal guide, include it so the session can follow the song’s structure.

02

Lyrics and structure. Share the lyrics and mark verses, chorus, bridge, or other sections. Notes about phrasing, pronunciation, or delivery help prepare the session.

03

References and direction. Send one or two reference tracks and describe the vocal tone or mood you want. References help explain the direction; the recording remains tailored to your song.

SESSION WORKFLOW

From preparation to organized vocal takes

01

Before recording. We review the song, material, references, and your goal, then agree on the session scope and timing.

02

Record the parts. Record lead vocals, doubles, and backing vocals as agreed for the song.

03

Organize takes. Select takes and prepare clean, clearly organized files for the next editing and mixing stage.

Recorded files are prepared for the next production stage. If you also need vocal editing, vocal mixing, or a full mix, we can discuss that separately after reviewing the session material.

Can I book mixing as well?

Yes. Vocal editing, vocal mixing or a full multitrack mix can be discussed after reviewing the session files.
