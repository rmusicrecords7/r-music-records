## Priority 2 — relevant studio directories

1. **Room For Sound** — https://roomforsound.com/
   - The directory says studios can list for free and allows search by city, type and budget.
   - Best first directory candidate for the Kyiv in-person studio listing.
   - Listing should describe the real Kyiv recording location and distinguish it from remote mixing/mastering available worldwide.
2. **AllStudios** — https://www.allstudios.co.uk/
   - International recording/mastering/studio directory with a registration route and listing verification.
   - It also operates a referral service that charges studios a commission, so check the terms before accepting referrals.
3. **SoundBetter** — https://www.soundbetter.com/
   - Marketplace with explicit categories for mixing engineers and mastering engineers, and a project/proposal workflow.
   - Treat it primarily as a lead channel, not merely a backlink. Create a profile only with accurate service details and work examples the owner is comfortable publishing.

Lower priority: SoundNet (https://soundnet.com/) appears to be a small public-beta marketplace; its current reach and lead quality are not yet established. Do not spend time on broad low-quality directories or paid link packages.

- Record each published listing URL and any real enquiries it produces; a backlink by itself is not a business result.
