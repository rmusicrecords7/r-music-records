# Competitor and conversion benchmark — 4 November 2026

## Scope and limits

This is a qualitative public-page benchmark, not a complete Google SERP ranking study or a keyword-volume report. Competitor pages were reviewed for visible offer structure, pricing transparency, delivery details, revision policies, and trust signals. Public prices may change and are not directly comparable where scope differs.

## Market observations

### France / French-language online mixing

The reviewed French providers make several purchase-decision details unusually explicit:

- Online Sound separates light, standard and premium mixing by track count (10 / 32 / 64 tracks), publishes prices (€99 / €149 / €199), and states that stereo mastering is included: https://www.online-sound.fr/tarifs/
- Audio-Prod publishes three tiers (€129 / €179 / €219), track limits (20 / 40 / 60), mastering inclusion, and three revisions: https://audio-prod.fr/service/mixage-audio/
- MixEnLigne publishes a per-song price (€159), two revision rounds, and a first-version target of five business days: https://mixenligne.fr/
- Studio AMTP distinguishes vocal-plus-instrumental mixing from multitrack mixing, lists starting prices, and offers adjacent services such as vocal editing and session cleanup: https://www.studioamtp.fr/services-a-distance/

### Germany / German-language online mastering

- Online-Mixing.de provides detailed mastering prices by format and project quantity, including stereo and stem-mastering options: https://www.online-mixing.de/preise/
- Faceline Records separates stereo mastering, hip-hop mix/master and online mixing, with concrete prices and scope: https://facelinerecords.de/online-mixing-mastering/

### Ukraine / Kyiv studio market

- NEWWAVE presents Kyiv location, hourly vocal recording, mix/master and arrangement as distinct offers, with starting prices and package contents: https://www.newwave.kiev.ua/en
- Checkpoint Studio distinguishes hourly tracking/reamping from project mixing/mastering and publishes rates: https://checkpoint.kiev.ua/en/studio
- Clutch's Ukraine audio-mixing category includes Kyiv providers such as Cavemen Records and Major Mixing. Directory listings can be a source of qualified discovery, but their ranking/verification model and commercial terms should be reviewed before investing time: https://clutch.co/ua/audio-production/audio-mixing

## Highest-value conversion gaps to resolve with verified owner facts

These details are prominent on competing pages but must not be invented:

1. **Revision policy:** number of included revision rounds and what counts as a revision for mixing, mastering, arrangement and Suno projects.
2. **Delivery expectations:** typical first-preview and final-delivery windows, with a clear caveat for project complexity/current workload.
3. **Technical deliverables:** available WAV bit depth/sample rate, MP3, instrumental/acapella versions, stems, streaming master variants, and whether these are included or optional.
4. **Scope boundaries:** maximum/typical track counts for each published mixing price; what the vocal-over-instrumental price includes.
5. **Suno input constraints:** what exact source formats are accepted and what can/cannot be promised when only a stereo render is available.
6. **Local recording booking:** session length, preparation requirements, and how the Kyiv session is confirmed. The published hourly price should remain aligned across language versions.

Until these facts are confirmed, retain the current honest “agree scope and timeline before work” wording. Do not copy competitor revision counts, delivery promises, file specs, or track limits.

## Recommended page changes once facts are confirmed

- Add a compact “Included in the price” block to mixing and mastering pages.
- Add a comparison table for multitrack mixing versus vocal mixing to a stereo instrumental, explaining the different source files and scope.
- Add a deliverables block to mastering (formats and streaming variants only if actually supplied).
- Add a revision/delivery policy shared across language versions.
- Add an explicit Suno assessment pathway: send a source link/file, review available material, then receive a scoped quote.
- Keep Kyiv vocal recording separate from remote mixing/mastering intent; do not imply that online production requires a studio visit.

## Important distinction

Competitor pricing is market context, not evidence that R Music Records should lower or raise its rates. The current published prices were not changed by this benchmark. No client numbers, testimonials, case studies, search volumes, rankings, or performance outcomes have been inferred.
