THE APPROACH

Ways to develop a Suno AI track

Available options depend on the source material. We first identify your goal and the limits of the audio, then agree on a realistic scope of work.

01

Review the source

Listen to the track and identify practical ways to develop it based on the files available.

02

Arrangement development

Plan changes to the structure, instrumental parts, and progression of the composition.

03

Editing individual parts

Available stems or other source material may allow parts to be replaced, added, or edited.

04

Mixing and mastering

Where the source and current state of the track allow, we can discuss final audio processing.

GETTING STARTED

What to send to get started

  • A link to the generation or the audio file itself.
  • If available, stems, MIDI, or your own musical material.
  • A short description of what you want to change and how the result should sound.

Not sure which files are needed? Send a short message describing what you have. We can review the material and decide on the next steps.
