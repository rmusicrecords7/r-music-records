Music Arrangement Services

  • Arrangement from scratch. Develop a melody, lyrics, demo, or concept into a complete musical arrangement.
  • Refining an existing arrangement. Improve the structure, instruments, individual parts, or overall sound while retaining elements that already work.
  • Complete re-arrangement. Create a new musical version based on your song or demo, preserving its central idea where needed.
  • Arrangement based on a reference. Use a track you like to define the mood, energy, instrumentation, or production direction, then create an original arrangement for your song rather than copying another recording.
  • Developing Suno AI music. Build on a promising AI-generated demo, change selected elements, or use it as a starting point for further production.

Arrangement Based on a Musical Reference

If you like the sound of a particular track, send it as a reference and explain what stands out to you: the instruments, rhythm, atmosphere, dynamics, or song structure. These details can guide an original arrangement tailored to your own song.

The reference helps establish the direction; the result is developed for your project rather than made as an exact copy of someone else's composition.

How It Works

  1. Send your demo, audio files, MIDI, or a description of your musical idea.
  2. Discuss the genre, target sound, references, and what should be kept or changed.
  3. Agree on the scope, price, and deadline before production begins.
  4. I work on the arrangement and we review the result and any agreed revisions.
