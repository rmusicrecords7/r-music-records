From Demo to Full Arrangement: What to Send and How the Process Works

Learn which demos, references, lyrics, tempo and key help turn a simple idea into a complete arrangement.

You do not need a finished studio recording to develop a demo into a complete arrangement. A clear musical idea and a short explanation of what should stay, change or stand out are enough to begin.

What to send to get started

  • A demo or voice memo. A melody, chord progression or rough vocal recorded on a phone can communicate the foundation of the song.
  • Lyrics and structure. If you have lyrics, mark the verse, chorus, bridge or other sections. If the structure is still open, we can define it together.
  • Reference tracks. Send one to three songs that communicate the mood, energy, groove or instrumental character you have in mind.
  • Tempo and key. Include the BPM and key if you know them. If not, they can be identified from the source material.
  • Source files. MIDI, stems or separate tracks are useful when available, but they are not always required for an initial review.

How to describe the sound you want

Rather than asking to copy a specific song, explain which elements you like: the drum weight, bass character, space, build-up, chorus energy or the balance of acoustic and electronic instruments. References guide the direction; the arrangement is developed for your own composition.

How the arrangement process works

  1. Material review. I listen to the demo, references and any available source files.
  2. Creative direction. We agree on the genre direction, structure, instrumentation, scope, timeline and price.
  3. Arrangement production. I develop the composition through rhythm, instrumental parts, transitions and dynamics.
  4. Final review. We discuss the agreed revisions and prepare the final files for delivery.

What affects the timeline and price?

The scope depends on the complexity of the song, the number of instrumental parts, whether the arrangement starts from scratch, the quality of the source material and any additional tasks. A reliable estimate is possible after reviewing the demo and references.
