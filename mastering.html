What Is Music Mastering?

Mastering is the final audio production stage before release. It refines the overall tonal balance, dynamics and loudness of a finished mix and helps prepare the track for digital distribution.

What Does the Service Include?

  • Tonal balance and frequency refinement
  • Dynamic control and loudness adjustment
  • Stereo image evaluation
  • Checks for unwanted peaks and technical issues
  • Final stereo file prepared for digital release
