THE APPROACH

What mastering focuses on

Mastering follows mixing. The focus is the finished stereo mix—not replacing a full mix session when the source problem requires work on individual tracks.

01

Tonal balance

Refining the overall frequency balance for the genre and the references you provide.

02

Dynamics and loudness

Controlling dynamic range and loudness without unnecessarily damaging transients.

03

Stereo image

Checking width, center stability, and mono compatibility.

04

Technical review

Checking peaks and preparing the agreed final audio file.

GETTING STARTED

What to send to get started

  • Your final stereo mix in the best available quality.
  • One or two references and a short description of your release goals.
  • The desired final file format, if already specified.

Not sure which files are needed? Send a short message describing what you have. We can review the material and decide on the next steps.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does mastering replace mixing?

No. Mastering works with a finished stereo mix; problems in individual tracks may require returning to mixing.

Can I send a reference track?

Yes. A reference helps define the target character and balance, but is not a template to copy exactly.

How much does mastering cost?

From $25 / 1,000 UAH per track. We agree on the details before work begins.
