FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about remote work, files, and project terms.

Do I need to visit the studio?

No. Projects are handled remotely: source files, references, and finished files are exchanged online.

What files should I send for mixing?

Usually, send separate tracks or stems exported from the same start point. A reference track and a short description of your target sound are also helpful.

How long does the work take?

Timing depends on the service, project complexity, and current workload. The deadline is agreed before work begins.

Can a Suno AI track be improved?

Options depend on the available files: a finished track, stems, an original composition, or other materials. The first step is to review the source material.

How is the final price determined?

For fixed-price services, use the rate shown in the price list. For more complex work or services marked “from,” the scope and final quote are agreed after reviewing the material.

How do I discuss an order?

The easiest way is to message me on WhatsApp or email with a demo or link and a short description of what you need.

Can I combine several services for one track?

Yes. Describe the project and share the files; we’ll agree on the workflow, scope, and price before starting.

What if I only have a finished audio file?

Send it for an initial review. The options depend on the file and the task: mixing usually requires separate tracks, while mastering may only need the finished mix.
