What matters

The key is to assess the whole musical context rather than one isolated parameter: the source material, genre, artistic intention and the way listeners will experience the finished track. A practical workflow from choosing a generation and exporting stems to replacing parts, recording vocals, mixing, mastering and release preparation.

A practical approach

Start by listening on more than one playback system and identifying the actual issue. Work methodically, keep the original files and compare changes at matched loudness. Treat technical figures as useful references, not substitutes for listening.

How to get a better result
