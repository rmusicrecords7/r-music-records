THE APPROACH

What mixing can include

Mixing is more than making things louder. It is the process of balancing vocals, rhythm, and musical details so they work together and support the intention of the song.

01

Balance and clarity

Level and frequency control so important elements remain clear without unnecessary harshness or overload.

02

Vocal processing

Equalization, dynamics, space, and effects chosen for the style and character of the vocal.

03

Space and depth

Reverb, delay, panning, and other tools that help each element sit naturally in the mix.

04

Final checks

Review of the stereo mix and preparation of a file for mastering when needed.

GETTING STARTED
