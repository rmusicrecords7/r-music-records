What Is Music Mixing?

Music mixing is the process of balancing vocals and instruments, shaping frequencies, controlling dynamics and creating depth. A professional mix helps every important element work together and prepares your track for mastering.

What Does the Mixing Service Include?

  • Vocal and instrument level balancing
  • Equalization and frequency control
  • Compression and dynamics processing
  • Reverb, delay and spatial effects
  • Vocal clarity and placement in the mix
  • A final stereo mix ready for mastering
