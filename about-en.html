ABOUT

Vitaliy — audio engineer and founder of R Music Records

I’ve worked in audio engineering for more than 15 years, helping artists turn musical ideas into cohesive productions.

My main focus is pop and rap. I work across mixing, mastering, arrangements and vocal production. I also help develop tracks created with Suno AI by reviewing the source files and identifying practical improvements to the arrangement and sound.

R Music Records works remotely. Source material, references and finished files are exchanged online. Before production starts, we agree on the task, scope, price and timeline.
