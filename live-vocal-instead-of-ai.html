Replacing an AI Vocal with a Real Performance: Why and How

Use an AI track as a demo, prepare an instrumental, record multiple takes and create a distinctive vocal performance.

A real vocal performance can add identity, emotion and a clear human contribution. Use an AI generation as a demo, keep its melody, tempo and structure, prepare an instrumental and record your own performance.

A safe workflow

Treat the generation as a guide, obtain an instrumental from stems or rebuild the arrangement, adapt the key to the singer’s range and record several lead and backing takes. Use a quiet room, keep a consistent microphone distance and leave recording headroom.
