Streaming Loudness and LUFS: How Loud Should Your Track Be?

Why −14 LUFS is not a mandatory upload target, how normalization works and why dynamics matter more than loudness wars.

The subject needs to be considered in its musical context: genre, artistic intent, source quality and the way the audience will hear the finished track. Why −14 LUFS is not a mandatory upload target, how normalization works and why dynamics matter more than loudness wars.

What matters

Start with a clear goal and check the result on more than one playback system. Compare versions at matched loudness, keep the original files and make changes methodically. Technical values are references, not universal rules.

A practical approach

For a professional result, agree on the scope, references and final file formats before the project begins.
